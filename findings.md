# Findings and implications

These findings interpret the [reproduced counts](results/summary.md) and [evidence matrix](results/evidence-matrix.md). They apply to eight purposively selected institutions and the official descriptions retrieved on 20 November 2026. Statements about further research are proposals, not observed athlete outcomes.

## 1 Competition access appears alongside several other support functions

The dataset codes competition access or delivery for four institutions: ISSA, JAAA, UTech, and UWI Mona. The remaining categories capture training, education for coaches and officials, facilities, finance, governance, welfare, and clean sport. Altogether, the corpus contains 19 assignments across eight functions. [Evidence](results/evidence-matrix.md).

**Interpretation.** A useful athlete-journey study should examine more than competition entry and results. The map gives a concrete starting list of service types to investigate around training and competition. It does not demonstrate that these services are sufficient, accessible, or coordinated.

**Research decision.** Build a future interview guide around the support an athlete actually received before and after leaving school. Ask which provider delivered each service and what evidence confirms the handoff. Institutional role descriptions alone cannot answer that question.

## 2 Tertiary institutions should not be treated as one uniform step

UTech and UWI Mona each have four coded functions in the selected descriptions. G C Foster has one coded function from a deliberately narrow source about coach development. These different counts reflect the reviewed evidence and coding scope. They are not rankings of the institutions. [S04](sources/source-register.md#s04), [S05](sources/source-register.md#s05), [S06](sources/source-register.md#s06).

**Interpretation.** Tertiary education can appear in a support map through direct athlete programmes or through the preparation of people who support athletes. Combining those functions into one generic university stage would hide a useful distinction.

**Research decision.** In a broader follow-up, keep athlete development and coaching-workforce development as separate analytical tracks. Obtain equivalent programme descriptions from each institution before comparing breadth, cost, participation, or outcomes. The current source imbalance makes those comparisons premature.

## 3 Cross-sport support belongs in the map, with its scope preserved

JADCO reports anti-doping workshops for high-school participants in 2026 and separately describes its testing programme. SDF's stated funding remit includes infrastructure and athlete welfare across sports. These sources justify including those functions, while leaving the athletics-specific reach unresolved. [S07](sources/source-register.md#s07), [S08](sources/source-register.md#s08), [S09](sources/source-register.md#s09).

**Interpretation.** Some support relevant to an athletics study comes from institutions whose remit extends beyond athletics. A model limited to track clubs and competition organisers would omit documented service types. Conversely, a cross-sport announcement cannot be converted into a track-and-field spending or participation estimate.

**Research decision.** A later analysis should request sport-specific eligibility and usage records where appropriate. Programme existence, eligibility, actual uptake, and outcomes should remain separate measures.

## 4 The elite-competition end of the map contains distinct institutional roles

JAAA describes a national athletics governing role. JOA describes national Olympic representation, and its September 2026 announcement identifies a scholarship mechanism for high-performance preparation. The corpus therefore records both governance and financial support without asserting how a particular athlete moves between them. [S03](sources/source-register.md#s03), [S10](sources/source-register.md#s10), [S11](sources/source-register.md#s11).

**Interpretation.** A map of organisations is insufficient to explain the process through which an athlete gains selection, receives support, or enters a major event. Those processes require their own rules and participant-level evidence.

**Research decision.** If the project expands, choose one defined transition and document the actual steps, responsible parties, and eligibility rules from current authoritative records. That would produce a process map. Drawing an arrow between organisations now would imply a relationship the present data has not established.
